# FurShield implementation plan

## Phase 1 — Platform foundation

Next.js App Router structure, responsive visual system, MongoDB connection, domain schemas, signed sessions, password hashing, validation, and role-based navigation.

## Phase 2 — Public discovery

Landing page, care library, veterinarian discovery, adoption catalog, product browsing and cart controls, About, Contact, and map/location experience.

## Phase 3 — Pet owner care space

Multiple pet profiles, edit/view/delete, health timelines, vaccinations and reminders, allergies/illness/treatments, signed gallery/document uploads, insurance files, appointment requests/cancellation, adoption interest, alerts, and ratings.

## Phase 4 — Veterinarian workspace

Professional profile and availability, booking status management, least-privilege access to booked pet histories, structured clinical notes, diagnosis, medication, lab and follow-up records.

## Phase 5 — Shelter workspace

Adoption profiles, current health/adoption status, feeding/grooming/medical care logs, adopter interest review, status coordination, and in-app updates.

## Phase 6 — Quality and delivery

Responsive/accessibility audit, authorization checks, lint/type/build validation, repeatable seed data, setup instructions, credentials, SRS traceability, and deployment readiness.
